Glass surface

Opt-in for Card, Dialog, DropdownMenu, Toast, Navbar and Alert. It's the same surface plus 80% opacity, a backdrop blur and a light rim (surface-glass-border). The 80% floor keeps body text above 9:1 and text-muted-glass above 4.5:1 over any backdrop. Under prefers-reduced-transparency it becomes surface-raised, and it's opaque in high-contrast.
